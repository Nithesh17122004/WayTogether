# WayTogether

**Share the same route.**

WayTogether is a private carpooling platform designed to connect people travelling along the same route. Its core model is based on independently planned journeys and reasonable sharing of journey expenses.

> This repository is the initial project foundation. Real-money operations must remain disabled until applicable legal, tax, insurance, and payment-provider reviews are complete.

## Product direction

- Website-first, responsive experience
- Pan-India technical architecture
- Premium mobility-tech brand identity
- Genuine pre-planned private carpool journeys
- No taxi-style commercial trip creation or driver-profit model

## Planned technology

- Frontend: React, Vite, Tailwind CSS
- Backend: Python, Django, Django REST Framework
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Maps: Leaflet with OpenStreetMap
- Testing: Pytest and Playwright

7. Payments and financial management (sandbox until approvals)
8. Communication, safety and trust
9. Admin panel and analytics
10. Testing, security and deployment

## Core product safeguards

- Drivers independently plan journeys and confirm they would travel without passengers.
- Passenger contributions are designed for eligible journey-expense sharing, not driver profit.
- Platform fees must be separately disclosed and used only after legal and tax review.
- Live payments and payouts must not be enabled until the operating model and provider are approved.
