# WayTogether Brand Guidelines

## Brand
**WayTogether**  
**Tagline:** Share the same route.

## Visual direction
Premium, modern mobility technology. Use a dark navy foundation with electric blue, violet and cyan accents, balanced by white and cool neutral text.

## Suggested design tokens
- Background: #050817
- Surface: #0B1230
- Elevated surface: #111B3D
- Primary blue: #287BFF
- Violet: #A43DFF
- Cyan: #18D5F2
- Main text: #F7F8FF
- Muted text: #A7B0CC
- Border: rgba(151, 170, 220, 0.18)

## UI principles
- High contrast, restrained gradients and subtle glow.
- Large, clear headings and readable body text.
- Strong focus states and keyboard support.
- Use gradients sparingly for primary actions and brand moments.
- Maintain a consistent 8px spacing rhythm and rounded card system.
- Design mobile-first; avoid dense desktop-only layouts.

The uploaded WayTogether logo is the visual source of truth. Add the actual logo asset to frontend/public/brand/ when the local asset is supplied to the project workflow.
